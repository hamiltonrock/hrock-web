FAQ

What is Hamilton Rock?

Hamilton Rock is a financial technology company built for U.S. ecommerce businesses. You use it to manage your business money in one simple platform. Hamilton Rock helps you grow your online business by giving you powerful financial accounts, AI cash-flow tools that help you optimize your cash flow, fast global payments, and access to fair and fast capital — all in one place designed for modern ecommerce.



Who is Hamilton Rock for?

Hamilton Rock is built for U.S. ecommerce businesses that sell goods and services online. You might sell through your own website, online stores, marketplaces, or payment platforms. Whether you run one store or several, Hamilton Rock gives you a simple way to manage your money, control your cash flow, and grow your business.


How do you get started?

Click “Join the Waitlist,” enter your email address and you’ll be first to know when Hamilton Rock goes live.

Your details are safe with us. We don’t spam, and we don’t share or sell your data.


What do you need to apply to Hamilton Rock?

Opening a Hamilton Rock account is simple and only requires a few basic details about your business.

You will typically need your legal business name, EIN, business address, a short description of what your business sells, and details for your owners or controllers.

Sometimes we may ask for supporting documents, such as your business formation documents (for example an LLC or incorporation certificate), identification for beneficial owners, and links to your online stores or marketplaces.

You only need to upload copies of these documents, and if you do not have everything ready, you can save your application and return later to finish it.

If you need help at any stage, our support team is always available to guide you through the process

Hamilton Rock partners with Stripe Payments Company for money transmission and account services. Hamilton Rock is a financial technology company, not a bank.

Banking services are provided by Fifth Third Bank, Member FDIC. Hamilton Rock Visa® Commercial Cards are issued by Celtic Bank pursuant to a license from Visa U.S.A. Inc.


How long does it take to open an account?

Most applications are reviewed quickly, and many accounts are opened within minutes. If we need additional documents or verification, the process may take up to 1–2 business days.


Why might an application not be approved?

Most businesses that meet our requirements are able to open a Hamilton Rock account. However, in some cases an application may not be approved.

This can happen if we are unable to verify the business or its owners, if the business does not meet our eligibility requirements, or if the business operates in industries that financial regulations do not allow us to support.

If additional information is needed, our team may contact you during the application process.

Hamilton Rock aims to make opening a business account simple, transparent, and fast, and we will always try to guide you through the process wherever possible.


What can I do with a Hamilton Rock account?

A Hamilton Rock account helps your Ecommerce business manage, move, and grow money, all in one place.

You can open up to 10 accounts instantly without visiting a bank branch and organise your money however you like. You can also create Cashboxes to set money aside for savings or future expenses.

Your balances can earn Earnings Rewards of 1.39%, which is over 46x higher than the average rate offered by the four largest U.S. banks.

You’ll also receive a Hamilton Rock spend card, allowing you to control spending and issue up to 20 cards instantly for your team. Every eligible purchase earns 1.5% cashback.

Hamilton Rock also connects to your online stores and payment platforms, automating payout reconciliation and reduce days of finance admin.

Over time, you will also be able to send international payments instantly around the world, all from the same platform designed specifically for Ecommerce businesses.
