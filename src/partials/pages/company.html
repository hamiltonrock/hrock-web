Our story

Inspired by Alexander Hamilton

In 1789, Founding Father Alexander Hamilton laid the foundations of America’s financial system, expanding economic prosperity to all and powering the growth of a young nation.

Today, Hamilton Rock carries that mission forward, re-founding finance for modern Ecommerce entrepreneurs with fairer access, smarter tools, and next-generation financial services, restoring opportunity and growth where traditional banks have failed to keep up with the needs of the digital economy.

Built by Founders Who’ve Been There

Hamilton Rock is the platform we wished existed — built by founders, for founders.
