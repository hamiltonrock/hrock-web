FAQ

Does Hamilton Rock charge monthly fees?

No. Hamilton Rock charges $0 monthly fees, $0 minimum balance fees, $0 account maintenance fees, and $0 transaction fees. Hamilton Rock is free to use, so you can focus on running and growing your business without worrying about hidden costs.

The only fee you may encounter is a charge from an ATM provider if you withdraw cash from their machine.


Do you need a minimum balance?

No. You do not need to keep a minimum balance in your Hamilton Rock account. You can hold any amount, even $0, and you will not be charged a fee. Hamilton Rock is designed to be flexible and accessible, whether you are just starting your ecommerce business or already growing fast


How does Hamilton Rock make money?

Hamilton Rock earns revenue mainly from card interchange, which is a small fee paid by the merchant’s bank whenever you use your Hamilton Rock card to make a purchase.

This means you can use Hamilton Rock’s banking services without monthly fees, minimum balances, or hidden charges while we earn a small portion of the payment network fee when your card is used.


Are there any fees?

Hamilton Rock is designed to be simple and transparent with no platform fees.

There are no monthly fees, no minimum balance requirements, and no hidden charges for using your account.

If a service ever involves a partner fee (for example an ATM operator), it will always be clearly shown before you complete the transaction.


Do you charge overdraft fees?

No. Hamilton Rock does not charge overdraft fees.

If there are not enough funds in your account to complete a transaction, the payment will simply not be processed.

This helps you stay in control of your spending and avoid unexpected charges.


Do you charge ATM fees?

Hamilton Rock does not charge ATM fees for using your card.

However, some ATM operators may charge their own fee for using their machines. If this applies, the fee will be shown on the ATM screen before you confirm the withdrawal.
