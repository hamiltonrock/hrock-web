FAQ

How do I fund my Hamilton Rock account?

You can fund your Hamilton Rock account in several easy ways. You can transfer money from another bank account using ACH, send a domestic wire transfer, or add funds using a card, Apple Pay, or Google Pay.

You can also receive payouts directly from your Ecommerce stores and payment platforms, such as Stripe, Shopify, Amazon, PayPal, and others.

Once your account is funded, you can start using your Hamilton Rock spend card and instantly earn 1.5% cashback on eligible purchases, helping your business earn money back on everyday spending.

Banking services are provided by Fifth Third Bank, N.A., Member FDIC. Hamilton Rock is a financial technology company, not a bank.

What is a Hamilton Rock Spend Card?

A Hamilton Rock Spend Card is a commercial business card that works like a debit card.

When you make a purchase, the money is paid directly from your Hamilton Rock account balance, rather than borrowing money like a credit card.

We call it a Spend Card because Hamilton Rock is a financial technology company and not a bank, but it works the same way most businesses use a debit card.

You can issue up to 20 cards instantly for your team, control spending for each card, and earn 1.5% cashback on eligible purchases.

Hamilton Rock partners with Stripe Payments Company for money transmission services and account services with funds held at Fifth Third Bank, N.A., Member FDIC. Hamilton Rock Spend Card Visa® Prepaid Cards are issued by Celtic Bank.

Are Hamilton Rock accounts real bank accounts?

Yes. When you open a Hamilton Rock account, you are opening a bank account held at our partner bank, Fifth Third Bank, in your business’s name. You can receive payouts, send ACH transfers and wires, and move money just like you would with a regular business checking account.

Because Hamilton Rock is a financial technology company and not a bank, we do not label these as “checking accounts.” Instead, we simply call them Hamilton Rock accounts, while your funds are securely held with our partner bank.


Are my funds protected?

Yes. Your funds are securely held at our partner bank, Fifth Third Bank, Member FDIC. Your Hamilton Rock accounts are eligible for FDIC pass-through deposit insurance of up to $250,000 per business entity, provided the applicable FDIC requirements are met.

This means your money is protected in the same way deposits are protected at many traditional banks, giving you the safety and security you expect while managing your finances through Hamilton Rock.

Funds held in Financial Accounts are deposited at one or more FDIC-insured depository institutions. Subject to the satisfaction of applicable FDIC requirements for pass-through insurance, deposits are eligible for FDIC insurance coverage up to $250,000 per depositor, per insured depository institution, for each ownership category, in accordance with the rules and regulations of the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation (FDIC).

Hamilton Rock Financial Services Corp and Stripe are financial technology companies and are not FDIC-insured depository institutions. FDIC deposit insurance protects against the failure of an FDIC-insured depository institution where deposits are held.
