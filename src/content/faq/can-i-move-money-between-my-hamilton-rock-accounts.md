question: "Can I move money between my Hamilton Rock accounts?"
order: 60
tags:
  - "Payments & Transfers"

Yes. You can instantly move money between your Hamilton Rock accounts and Cashboxes, helping you organise and manage your business finances with ease.

For example, you can quickly move funds between accounts for spending, saving, or budgeting, or set money aside in a Cashbox to keep it separate from your day-to-day operating funds.

You can also make payments directly from your accounts or Cashboxes, giving you flexibility and full control over how your Ecommerce business manages and moves money.

Banking services are provided by Fifth Third Bank, N.A., Member FDIC. Hamilton Rock is a financial technology company, not a bank.
