---
question: "What happens if a payment fails or is returned?"
order: 80
tags:
  - "Payments & Transfers"
---

If a payment cannot be completed, it will usually be returned to your Hamilton Rock account.

This can happen for a few common reasons, such as incorrect account details, insufficient funds, or the receiving bank rejecting the payment.

If a payment fails, you will see the status and details in your Hamilton Rock dashboard, so you can quickly understand what happened and resend the payment if needed.

Our goal is to make payments clear, transparent, and easy to manage, so you always know where your money is and what action to take.

If you need help resolving a payment issue, our support team is always available to assist you

Banking services are provided by Fifth Third Bank, N.A., Member FDIC. Hamilton Rock is a financial technology company, not a bank.
