Opening a Hamilton Rock account is simple and only requires a few basic details about your business.

You will typically need your legal business name, EIN, business address, a short description of what your business sells, and details for your owners or controllers.

Sometimes we may ask for supporting documents, such as your business formation documents (for example an LLC or incorporation certificate), identification for beneficial owners, and links to your online stores or marketplaces.

You only need to upload copies of these documents, and if you do not have everything ready, you can save your application and return later to finish it.

If you need help at any stage, our support team is always available to guide you through the process

Hamilton Rock partners with Stripe Payments Company for money transmission and account services. Hamilton Rock is a financial technology company, not a bank.
