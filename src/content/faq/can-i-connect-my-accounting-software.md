Yes. Hamilton Rock will allow you to connect popular accounting software, including QuickBooks, Xero, FreshBooks, and other major platforms.

Once connected, Hamilton Rock can automatically send key financial data — starting with your sales data — directly to your accounting software. This helps keep your books accurate and up to date without manual work.

By automating this process, Hamilton Rock can save you hours of finance admin, reduce errors, and make it easier for your accountant or bookkeeper to prepare your accounts.

Banking services are provided by Fifth Third Bank, N.A., Member FDIC. Hamilton Rock is a financial technology company, not a bank.
