When you sell online, the amount deposited into your bank account is usually different from your total sales.

This is because payment platforms such as Stripe, Shopify Payments, Amazon, and PayPal deduct certain items before sending the final payout to your bank. These can include payment processing fees, refunds, returns, chargebacks, marketplace commissions, currency conversion fees, or rolling reserves.

Because of this, the final payout that reaches your bank account is often lower than your gross sales, which can make reconciliation difficult.
