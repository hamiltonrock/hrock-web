---
question: "Can I add my existing bank accounts to Hamilton Rock?"
order: 140
tags:
  - "Connecting Platforms & Financial Visibility"
---

Yes. Hamilton Rock will allow you to securely connect your existing bank accounts from many major banks, such as Bank of America, Chase, Citi, Wells Fargo, and others.

Once connected, you will be able to view balances and transactions from those accounts directly inside your Hamilton Rock dashboard, giving you a clearer picture of your overall finances in one place.

This makes it easier to track your cash across multiple accounts, plan upcoming payments, and manage your Ecommerce business finances more efficiently.

Banking services are provided by Fifth Third Bank, N.A., Member FDIC. Hamilton Rock is a financial technology company, not a bank.
