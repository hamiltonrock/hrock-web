Yes. Hamilton Rock lets you connect your Ecommerce stores and payment platforms in just a few clicks.

Setup takes less than 30 seconds and usually only requires two simple steps. There is no technical knowledge required and no need to deal with connectors, APIs, or complicated integrations.

Once connected, Hamilton Rock can automatically see all your platform KPIs, track payouts from your stores and payment platforms, helping you keep your finances organised and making it easier to manage and reconcile your Ecommerce income, all from one platform.

Banking services are provided by Fifth Third Bank, N.A., Member FDIC. Hamilton Rock is a financial technology company, not a bank.
