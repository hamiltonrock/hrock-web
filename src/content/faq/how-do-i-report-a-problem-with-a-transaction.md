If you notice a problem with a transaction, you can contact the Hamilton Rock support team right away and we will help investigate it.

You can reach us through live chat (IM) in your dashboard, by email, or by phone. Our team will review the transaction, explain what happened, and guide you on the next steps if action is required.

Our goal is to resolve issues quickly and transparently, so you always feel confident that your Ecommerce business finances are in safe hands.

Banking services are provided by Fifth Third Bank, N.A., Member FDIC. Hamilton Rock Spend Card Visa® Prepaid Cards are issued by Celtic Bank. Hamilton Rock is a financial technology company, not a bank.
