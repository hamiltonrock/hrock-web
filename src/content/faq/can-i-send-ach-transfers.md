question: "Can I send ACH transfers?"
order: 40
tags:
  - "Payments & Transfers"
---

Yes. You can send and receive ACH transfers directly from your Hamilton Rock account to and from other bank accounts across the United States.

ACH transfers are commonly used for paying suppliers, contractors, and business expenses, as well as receiving payments from customers or other businesses.

You can create, track, and manage ACH payments directly from your Hamilton Rock dashboard, making it easy to move money and stay in control of your Ecommerce business finances. Standard ACH takes between 1-3 business days, whereas Same Day ACH typically arrives the same business day.

Banking services are provided by Fifth Third Bank, N.A., Member FDIC. Hamilton Rock is a financial technology company, not a bank.
