question: "Can I use Hamilton Rock for personal banking?"
order: 350
tags:
  - "Accounts & Eligibility"

No. Hamilton Rock is designed specifically for Ecommerce businesses, so accounts are only available for registered businesses and business use.

This allows us to build tools tailored to the needs of Ecommerce founders, such as managing payouts from online stores, organising business funds, tracking transactions, and simplifying financial admin.

Hamilton Rock is built to help Ecommerce businesses run their finances more efficiently, all from one platform.

Banking services are provided by Fifth Third Bank, N.A., Member FDIC. Hamilton Rock is a financial technology company, not a bank.
