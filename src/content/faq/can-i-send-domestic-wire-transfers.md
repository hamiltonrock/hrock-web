Yes. You can send and receive domestic wire transfers directly from your Hamilton Rock account to and from other U.S. bank accounts.

Wire transfers are useful when you need to move larger amounts of money quickly, such as paying suppliers, making business purchases, or transferring funds between banks.

You can create and manage wire transfers directly from your Hamilton Rock dashboard, giving your Ecommerce business a fast and reliable way to move money when it matters most. Domestic Wire Transfers: usually the same business day, often within hours if sent before bank cut-off times

Banking services are provided by Fifth Third Bank, N.A., Member FDIC. Hamilton Rock is a financial technology company, not a bank.
