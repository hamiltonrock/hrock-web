A Hamilton Rock Spend Card is a commercial business card that works like a debit card.

When you make a purchase, the money is paid directly from your Hamilton Rock account balance, rather than borrowing money like a credit card.

We call it a Spend Card because Hamilton Rock is a financial technology company and not a bank, but it works the same way most businesses use a debit card.

You can issue up to 20 cards instantly for your team, control spending for each card, and earn 1.5% cashback on eligible purchases.

Hamilton Rock partners with Stripe Payments Company for money transmission services and account services with funds held at Fifth Third Bank, N.A., Member FDIC. Hamilton Rock Spend Card Visa® Prepaid Cards are issued by Celtic Bank.
