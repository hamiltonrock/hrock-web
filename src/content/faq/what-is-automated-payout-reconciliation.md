Automated payout reconciliation is a feature that helps your Ecommerce business match the money you receive in your bank account with the sales recorded in your online stores.

When you sell online, the payout you receive from platforms like Stripe, Shopify Payments, Amazon, or PayPal is usually different from your total sales because of processing fees, refunds, chargebacks, marketplace fees, and other adjustments.

Hamilton Rock automatically compares three key data sources — your store orders, your payment platform settlement reports, and the deposit that arrives in your bank account. This is often called a “three-way match.”

By automatically matching these records, Hamilton Rock helps you see exactly where every dollar went, reduce reconciliation errors, and save hours of finance admin every week

Banking services are provided by Fifth Third Bank, N.A., Member FDIC. Hamilton Rock is a financial technology company, not a bank.
