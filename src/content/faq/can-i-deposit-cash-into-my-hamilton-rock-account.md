Most Ecommerce businesses receive payments through online stores, marketplaces, and payment platforms, which can send payouts directly to your Hamilton Rock account. You can also fund your account using ACH transfers, domestic wire transfers, cards, Apple Pay, or Google Pay.

This digital-first approach makes it faster and easier to manage your business finances without needing to visit a bank branch.

Banking services are provided by Fifth Third Bank, N.A., Member FDIC. Hamilton Rock is a financial technology company, not a bank.

‍Hamilton Rock is built for digital-first Ecommerce businesses, so we currently do not support cash deposits.
