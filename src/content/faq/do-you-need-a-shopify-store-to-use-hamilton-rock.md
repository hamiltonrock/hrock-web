---
question: "Do you need a Shopify store to use Hamilton Rock?"
order: 450
tags:
  - "Ecommerce Compatibility"
---

No. You do not need a Shopify store to use Hamilton Rock. You can run your business through WooCommerce, Wix, Amazon, Etsy, eBay, or other marketplaces and payment platforms.

Whether you operate one store or several, run a dropshipping business, or sell through multiple marketplaces, Hamilton Rock is designed to support all types of ecommerce businesses.
