The Hamilton Rock Spend Card is designed to give Ecommerce businesses simple, secure control over their spending.

You can issue up to 20 cards instantly for your team, making it easy to manage expenses across your business. Each card can be controlled directly from your Hamilton Rock dashboard, where you can set spending limits, restrict certain merchant categories, or freeze and unfreeze cards instantly.

Every time you use your card for eligible purchases, you earn 1.5% cashback, helping your business earn money back on everyday spending.

You can also track transactions in real time, giving you clear visibility and control over how your Ecommerce business spends money.

Hamilton Rock partners with Stripe Payments Company for money transmission services and account services with funds held at Fifth Third Bank, N.A., Member FDIC. Hamilton Rock Spend Card Visa® Prepaid Cards are issued by Celtic Bank.
