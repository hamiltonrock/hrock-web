No. Hamilton Rock does not sell your personal data. Protecting your information is not only a legal requirement, but also part of how we believe financial services should operate.

Hamilton Rock follows strict privacy laws, including the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (GLBA), which protects your non-public personal information. We only use your data to operate your account, provide services, and meet legal and regulatory requirements.

Your data is never sold and is only shared with trusted partners when necessary to operate your account and deliver our services.

You can also request access to your personal information or ask us to correct it at any time. See our Privacy Policy for more details.
