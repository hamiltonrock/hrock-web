question: "Why might an application not be approved?"
order: 260
tags:
  - "Get Started (General)"
---

Most businesses that meet our requirements are able to open a Hamilton Rock account. However, in some cases an application may not be approved.

This can happen if we are unable to verify the business or its owners, if the business does not meet our eligibility requirements, or if the business operates in industries that financial regulations do not allow us to support.

If additional information is needed, our team may contact you during the application process.

Hamilton Rock aims to make opening a business account simple, transparent, and fast, and we will always try to guide you through the process wherever possible.
