order: 200

Hamilton Rock provides a business spend card that lets you pay for everyday business expenses directly from your account.

You earn 1.5% cashback on qualified purchases and is automatically credited back to your account the following month.

Qualified business purchases are primarily transactions such as cash withdrawals, cash-equivalent transactions, balance transfers, and similar non-purchase transactions.

For full details, please see Cashback terms.

Hamilton Rock partners with Stripe Payments Company for money transmission services and account services with funds held at Fifth Third Bank, N.A., Member FDIC. Hamilton Rock Spend Card Visa® Prepaid Cards are issued by Celtic Bank.
