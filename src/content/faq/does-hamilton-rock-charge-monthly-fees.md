question: "Does Hamilton Rock charge monthly fees?"
order: 360

No. Hamilton Rock charges $0 monthly fees, $0 minimum balance fees, $0 account maintenance fees, and $0 transaction fees. Hamilton Rock is free to use, so you can focus on running and growing your business without worrying about hidden costs.

The only fee you may encounter is a charge from an ATM provider if you withdraw cash from their machine.
