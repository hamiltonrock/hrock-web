---
question: "What type of business can open a Hamilton Rock account?"
order: 320
tags:
  - "Accounts & Eligibility"
---

Hamilton Rock accounts are available for registered U.S. businesses that sell goods or services online.

This includes businesses such as Sole Proprietorships, Limited Liability Companies (LLCs), Corporations (C-Corps or S-Corps), Partnerships, and other legally registered business entities.

Hamilton Rock is built specifically for Ecommerce businesses, including sellers operating on platforms such as Shopify, Amazon, Etsy, eBay, WooCommerce, Wix, and other online marketplaces.

Whether you run one online store or multiple stores across different platforms, Hamilton Rock helps you manage your business finances in one place.

Banking services are provided by Fifth Third Bank, N.A., Member FDIC. Hamilton Rock is a financial technology company, not a bank.
