Hamilton Rock is a financial technology company built for U.S. ecommerce businesses. You use it to manage your business money in one simple platform. Hamilton Rock helps you grow your online business by giving you powerful financial accounts, AI cash-flow tools that help you optimize your cash flow, fast global payments, and access to fair and fast capital — all in one place designed for modern ecommerce.
