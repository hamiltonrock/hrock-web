order: 420

You can earn Earnings Rewards on the money you hold in your Hamilton Rock accounts. These rewards work similar to interest or APY at a traditional bank, helping your balance grow while your money sits in your account.

Your Earnings Rewards are calculated daily on your eligible balance and paid into your account monthly as a cash reward.

Because Hamilton Rock is a financial technology company and not a bank, we do not label these as interest or APY. Instead, we call them Earnings Rewards, while your funds are securely held with our partner bank.

See Earnings Rewards terms for full details.
