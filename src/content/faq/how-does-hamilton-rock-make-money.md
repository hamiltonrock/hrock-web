question: "How does Hamilton Rock make money?"
order: 380
tags:
  - "Pricing & Fees"
---

Hamilton Rock earns revenue mainly from card interchange, which is a small fee paid by the merchant’s bank whenever you use your Hamilton Rock card to make a purchase.

This means you can use Hamilton Rock’s banking services without monthly fees, minimum balances, or hidden charges while we earn a small portion of the payment network fee when your card is used.
