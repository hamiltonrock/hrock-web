---
question: "Can international founders open a Hamilton Rock account?"
order: 340
tags:
  - "Accounts & Eligibility"
---

Yes, in many cases international founders can open a Hamilton Rock account if they have a U.S.-registered business.

For example, if you live outside the United States but have formed a U.S. company (such as a Delaware LLC or Corporation) with a valid EIN, you may still be able to apply.

During the application process, we may ask for additional verification information to confirm your business and identity.

Hamilton Rock is designed to support modern Ecommerce businesses, including founders who operate globally while running a U.S.-registered company.

Banking services are provided by Fifth Third Bank, N.A., Member FDIC. Hamilton Rock is a financial technology company, not a bank.
