---
question: "Where must my business be based to open an account?"
order: 330
tags:
  - "Accounts & Eligibility"
---

To open a Hamilton Rock account, your business must be registered in the United States and have a valid U.S. Employer Identification Number (EIN).

Hamilton Rock currently supports U.S.-registered businesses that operate Ecommerce stores or sell products and services online.

If your business is registered outside the United States, you may not yet be able to open an account, but we hope to support more international businesses in the future as Hamilton Rock grows.

Banking services are provided by Fifth Third Bank, N.A., Member FDIC. Hamilton Rock is a financial technology company, not a bank.
