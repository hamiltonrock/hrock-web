---
question: "How does the 1.5% cashback on cards work?"
order: 440
tags:
  - "Rewards & Benefits"
---

When you use your Hamilton Rock card (physical or virtual) for eligible business purchases, you earn 1.5% cashback on qualified purchases. Cashback is calculated on completed transactions and paid into your Hamilton Rock account each month.

The main items that do not qualify for cashback include cash withdrawals, transfers, wallet funding, refunds, and other cash-like transactions.

See Cashback terms for the full list of exclusions.
